Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 21, 2026

Instagram File Support ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our data practices.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The Extension operates entirely locally in your browser and does not:

  • Track your browsing activity
  • Collect personal information
  • Store files on our servers
  • Use cookies or analytics
  • Share data with third parties

How the Extension Works

When you upload a file using the Extension:

  1. The file is sent directly from your browser to Filebin.net, a third-party file hosting service
  2. Filebin generates a shareable link, which the Extension inserts into your Instagram message
  3. The Extension does not store, process, or have access to your files

Third-Party Services

Files uploaded through the Extension are stored on Filebin.net, which is an independent service with its own privacy policy and terms of service. Please review Filebin's Privacy Policy to understand how they handle uploaded files.

Permissions

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • downloads — To enable file downloads within Instagram
  • host permissions for instagram.com — To inject the file upload button into Instagram's interface
  • host permissions for filebin.net — To upload files to Filebin's servers

These permissions are used solely to provide the Extension's functionality. No data is collected or transmitted to us.

Data Security

Since the Extension does not collect or store any data, there is no user data at risk. All file transfers occur directly between your browser and Filebin's servers.

Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
